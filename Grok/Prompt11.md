**Here’s a list of notable talent/staffing agencies specializing in AI** (including machine learning, generative AI, data science, AI engineering, research, and related roles). These handle contract, contract-to-hire, permanent, and executive placements. The AI talent market is highly competitive, so many focus on specialized vetting, technical assessments, and access to global/nearshore talent.

### Major National/Well-Known Agencies with Strong AI Practices
- **Insight Global** — Excellent for high-volume AI staffing and professional services, including AI engineers, ML engineers, GenAI specialists, and infrastructure roles. Supports enterprise-scale needs with quick placements.
- **Robert Half Technology** — Reliable for AI/ML roles alongside broader tech staffing, with AI-powered matching tools. Strong national presence.
- **TEKsystems** — Large IT staffing firm with significant AI/ML and data capabilities; good for speed and volume in tech hubs.
- **KORE1** — Specializes in placing pre-vetted AI and ML engineers (contract, contract-to-hire, direct hire) with high retention rates and fast time-to-hire.
- **Apex Systems** — Broad tech/engineering staffing with strong support for AI, data analytics, and software engineering roles.

### Specialized/Boutique AI-Focused Agencies
- **MSH (TalentMsh)** — Top-rated for AI/ML engineer staffing, with nearshore options and specialized tools like Aeon Hire.
- **Harnham** — Data & AI recruitment specialist (permanent and contract), strong in AI engineering, ML, and analytics leadership. Global reach with U.S. focus.
- **People In AI** — Dedicated exclusively to AI, ML, and data science hiring across the U.S. and Canada. Focuses on technical and cultural fit for startups to enterprises.
- **AI Staffing Ninja** — Hands-on agency for AI engineers (NLP, computer vision, MLOps, etc.), with fast project-based and full-time placements.
- **DeepRec.ai** — Deep tech and AI specialist (AI research, GenAI, computer vision, infrastructure). Targets advanced roles for leading AI companies.
- **Razoroo** — Specialized in AI and data science contract placements, particularly in tech hubs.

### Executive Search & Leadership-Focused
- **Alpha Apex Group** — Strategic AI executive search and consulting for leadership roles (e.g., Chief AI Officers).
- **Talentfoot** — Recognized for high-success AI executive placements.
- **Heidrick & Struggles**, **Korn Ferry**, and others like **Christian & Timbers** or **Cowen Partners** — Traditional executive firms with strong AI practices.

### Other Notable Mentions
- **GoGloby** (nearshore U.S./Latin America focus), **NextPath AI**, **CalTek Staffing**, **Peterson Technology Partners**, and **Zilo AI** (AI talent + data services).
- AI-powered platforms/tools like **Eightfold AI** (talent intelligence) and **Fetcher** are often used by agencies or in-house teams.

**Tips for choosing**:
- For **specialized technical AI/ML/GenAI roles** — Prioritize boutiques like People In AI, Harnham, DeepRec.ai, or KORE1.
- For **high-volume or enterprise needs** — Insight Global, TEKsystems, or Robert Half often scale well.
- Many offer nearshore/offshore options (e.g., Latin America) due to U.S. talent shortages.
- Verify technical vetting processes, retention rates, and niche expertise. Check reviews on Clutch, Glassdoor, or industry lists, as the space evolves rapidly.
